Mine Twist — Delete your data

Mine Twist is published by NomNom Games. Last updated: 2026-10-02

Mine Twist has no accounts, and the developer keeps no profile of you. This page lists every place data from the app can be, and how to delete it.

1. Your game progress (on your phone)

Scores, coins, levels and settings are stored only on your phone. To delete them, open Android Settings → Apps → Mine Twist → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. They are deleted at once and cannot be recovered. The developer has no copy.

2. Purchase records

If you bought something in the app, a record of the purchase (which item, when, and an anonymous app user ID; never your name or payment details) is kept by our purchase processor, RevenueCat, so that "Restore purchases" works.

To have that record deleted, email nomnomgameorg@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data - Mine Twist" and the Google Play order number of your purchase (it starts with GPA.; find it in your Google Play purchase history or the receipt email). We delete the record within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Google Play keeps its own order history under your Google account, as Google's terms require; manage it at payments.google.com. Your purchases themselves stay with your Google account.

3. Advertising data

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. To delete or reset it, open Android Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → Delete advertising ID. To control or delete Google's ad data, visit Google My Ad Center.

Questions

Write to nomnomgameorg@gmail.com. See also the Mine Twist privacy policy.
