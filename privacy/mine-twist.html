Mine Twist — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

Mine Twist ("the app") is a mobile game published by NomNom Games. This policy explains what data is processed when you use it.

Data the developer collects

The app does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to the developer. Your game progress — scores, coins, unlocks and settings — is stored only on your own device and is never uploaded.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID and related signals in order to serve and measure ads, and may share it with advertising partners. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's partner information. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, and opt out of personalised ads at Google My Ad Center.

Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the app.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. The app receives entitlement information (what you bought) — never your payment card details. Purchase validation may be handled by RevenueCat (privacy policy).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from them.

Data retention and deletion

Because game data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it. For any privacy question or request, contact nomnomgameorg@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

Any update will be published at this same URL with a revised "last updated" date.
